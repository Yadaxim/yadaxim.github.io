// About

I trained as a physicist — a BSc in Physics, then an MSc in Artificial Intelligence — and physics never really left. It is still how I look at things: find the model, respect the uncertainty, trust what the data can actually support.

Since then I've spent a decade in production — data engineering, backend systems, and applied AI — for startups in Mexico, the UK, and Slovenia. I like code that is clean, light, and elegant, with a healthy respect for schemas, and I work best on small async teams where breadth matters.

These days I build LLM-native tools — fast, precise, and opinionated about what gets shipped. And after a lifetime of software, I've started getting my hands dirty with hardware: microcontrollers, a soldering iron, and that satisfying moment when code moves something real.

Based in Slovenia. Working remotely. Open to interesting problems.
